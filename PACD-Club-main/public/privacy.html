Privacy notice

Last reviewed: 26 July 2026

Information we collect

Collaboration applications may include your name, professional role, institution, country or region, institutional email, research interests, proposal and an optional PDF CV.

How information is used

The information is used only to assess potential research collaboration, communicate about your application and administer the PACD Research Club.

Access and storage

Applications and CV files are stored in Supabase. Application records are accessible only to the applicant through a verified email session and to approved administrators. CV files are stored privately.

Contact

For access, correction or deletion requests, email angleclosureclub@163.com.
